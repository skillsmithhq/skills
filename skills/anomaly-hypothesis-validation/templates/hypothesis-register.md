# Hypothesis register

Write the test and its refutation condition **before** looking at the result.

| ID | Anomaly | Hypothesis (testable statement) | Type: data / normal variation / business / external | Test (script and parameters, or evidence source) | Refuted if | Result (numbers, evidence ID) | Evidence for | Evidence against | Status: supported / not supported / untested / unexplained | Confidence (with reason) | Missing evidence (item, holder) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Minimum: three hypotheses per anomaly, including one data-quality hypothesis.
